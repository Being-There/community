# Frequently asked questions

### Can my family use Being There now?
Not through this community project yet. The community edition is under development and has no public installer or hosted service.

### Is this open source?
The community effort intends to release open-source software. The community application repository remains private until source, history, issue metadata, security, permissions, installation, and restore checks are approved. This public outreach repository is separate.

### Do I need to share my family's memories to help?
No. Please **do not** post private memories, medical information, photographs, recordings, or personal contact information to public GitHub.

### Do I need a GitHub account?
To post GitHub Issues or Discussions, yes. Browsing public project material does not require an account. We have not yet established a separate private signup channel.

### Is Being There a nonprofit? Can I donate?
Not currently. We are exploring fiscal sponsorship or a nonprofit structure. We are not accepting donations through this project or promising tax deductions.

### Does this diagnose, treat, or measure dementia?
No. Being There is focused on family connection and enjoyment, not diagnosis, testing, or treatment.

### Who owns a family's data?
Our **goal** is family-controlled hosting and portable archives. Those capabilities still require implementation and independent verification before a community release.
