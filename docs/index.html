Being There is a family-centered project exploring how familiar photographs, voices, and stories can help people connect and enjoy meaningful moments together.

It began as a personal family project. We're working toward a separate, privacy-focused community edition that families can run and control for themselves.

Early-stage project. We're welcoming founding supporters. The community application is not yet available for general use, and we are not accepting donations.

What we're working toward

Memories are for enjoying, not testing. Nobody needs to remember a name, answer a question, or finish a story to take part. Families should control their own access, sharing, and archives.

Help build Being There

We welcome contributors in accessibility, caregiving experience, design, engineering, privacy and security, documentation, and nonprofit development. You don't need to share any personal family information to participate.

Offer to help on GitHub Ways to contribute

Before posting: GitHub issues are public and require an account. Please don't post medical details, private family stories, photos, recordings, email addresses, or other personal information.

Our commitment

We're pursuing open-source software and exploring a mission-led nonprofit initiative or fiscal sponsorship. We are not currently a nonprofit, are not soliciting donations, and do not offer medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
