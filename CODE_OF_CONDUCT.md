# Community expectations

Being There is about respectful, accessible family connection. We welcome contributors with different backgrounds, skills and experiences.

- Be respectful and constructive. Disagree with ideas without attacking people.
- Do not pressure anyone to share diagnoses, caregiving histories, private family stories, photos, or recordings.
- Use fictional examples in public project conversations.
- Respect people's autonomy and avoid assumptions about their abilities.
- Do not disclose another person's private information or security vulnerabilities in public issues.
- Keep feedback actionable, especially when reviewing accessibility.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict participation when necessary under GitHub's moderation tools. This document does not establish a confidential reporting channel. If a sensitive issue arises, do not post details publicly; use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting when enabled for security issues.
