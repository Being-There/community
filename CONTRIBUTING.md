# Contribute to Being There

Thank you for considering helping. We are at the **founding-supporters** stage, not a general software release.

## Where help is useful

- **Caregiver and family experience:** review fictional scenarios and explain what feels comfortable, useful, or confusing. Do not share private medical or family information.
- **Accessibility and design:** large controls, clear language, screen readers, mobile usability, and low-pressure interactions.
- **Engineering:** privacy architecture, security, portability, tests, documentation, and beginner installation.
- **Community and nonprofit:** volunteer onboarding, governance, fiscal sponsorship research, and funding options.

## How to participate

1. Review the [project overview](README.md) and [privacy guidance](PRIVACY.md).
2. Open a public GitHub issue describing the **type of help you can offer** or an idea. A GitHub account is required.
3. Keep your issue about the work, not your identity or private circumstances. We do not need your email address or family stories.
4. Wait for maintainers to identify a suitable contribution before starting significant work.

**GitHub issues and comments are public and searchable.** Do not include credentials, vulnerability details, contact details, diagnoses, private media, or information about another person. For security concerns, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting if enabled; otherwise do not post exploit details publicly.

Please be respectful of families, people experiencing memory changes, and each person's autonomy. Avoid assumptions about diagnoses or abilities.

We will not ask volunteers to access the private family application or real family archives.
